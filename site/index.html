Angles used, and how often they've hit

Each is an ATS cover rate on closing lines over every regular-season game since 1999, recomputed every build. Out of 30 angles tested, these are the only ones that beat 50% in both eras. They overlap, so they do not add up: the leans above use how each exact combination actually covered. Treat them as tiebreakers: a 52% edge still loses almost half the time.

Line movement is usually the biggest edge in a pick'em league. If your league locks lines midweek and the real line moves afterward, the side the market moved toward is getting extra points at your league's number. Each point of movement is worth very roughly 2–3% of cover probability, more when it crosses 3 or 7. Enter your league's line on each game to use it.
